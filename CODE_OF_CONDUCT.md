# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Telekom Speedport Smart community a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that supports this community include:

- showing empathy and respect for different experiences and viewpoints
- giving and accepting constructive technical feedback
- focusing discussion on reproducible behavior and evidence
- accepting responsibility, apologizing, and learning from mistakes
- protecting the privacy of contributors and router owners

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- sexualized language, unwanted attention, or harassment
- trolling, insults, threats, or personal and political attacks
- publishing another person's private information without permission
- pressuring anyone to disclose router credentials or household network data
- behavior that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  community

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. They may edit or remove contributions that violate them and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Report conduct concerns to the maintainer
[@aarikmudgal](https://github.com/aarikmudgal). When confidentiality is
required, use the repository's
[private report form](https://github.com/aarikmudgal/ha-speedport-smart/security/advisories/new)
and identify the report as a conduct matter. Reports will be reviewed as
privately as practical. Retaliation against a reporter is not tolerated.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when a person publicly represents the
project or its community.
